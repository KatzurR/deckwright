# Deckwright

Deckwright builds printable cards for tabletop role-playing games and collects them in decks.

## Language

### Decks and cards

**Deck**:
A named collection of cards that share one card language and one set of display options.
_Avoid_: Set, collection

### Colour

**Card colour**:
The frame colour of every card in a deck. It is chosen once per deck, never per card.
_Avoid_: Card theme, frame theme, preset colour

**Accent colour**:
The colour of a card's title, labels, drop cap and picture background. It is derived from the card colour so that it stays readable on the parchment.
_Avoid_: Text colour, highlight colour

**School colour**:
The accent colour a spell card takes from its school of magic when the deck colours spells by school. It replaces the accent colour but not the card colour.
_Avoid_: Spell colour
